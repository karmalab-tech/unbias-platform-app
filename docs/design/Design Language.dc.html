03

Layout & shape

The dashboard is a single unscrolled frame divided into full-width bands. Bands are separated by a hairline, never by a gap or a card shadow.

Frame
1512 × 1134 (4:3) reference canvas, no page scroll. Gutter 56px left and right. In the app this becomes a max-width container with the same gutter.
Band rhythm
Vertical padding 30–44px per band, divided by a 1px ink/10% rule. Within a band, columns are separated the same way.
Spacing steps
6 · 9 · 14 · 22 · 30 · 44 · 56
Use gap, not margins, for sibling groups.

Radius

No shadows
Depth comes from tinted grounds and hairlines. There is no elevation scale — the only dark overlay in the system is the video scrim at rgba(18,16,12,.94).

05

Data display

All charts are the same bar: solid ink for approved, a 45° hatch for pending review, the remaining track showing how far the goal is. Three fills, no more.

Approved
#17150f
Pending review
#e3dac9 + 45° 1.6/4.4
Track / still needed
#eae1d3

Bar variants

Horizontal when the label is a word. Vertical when the label is a swatch or a number and there are more than about six categories. Bar height 11–12px horizontal; radius 4px (square and pill are available as a system-wide switch).

Labelled rows
{{ row.label }}
{{ row.txt }}
Swatch columns
{{ col.txt }}
Numbers, not percentages
Counts are absolute, comma-grouped, tabular-nums. Goals drive bar length but are not printed next to every row — they made the charts unreadable.
One legend per band
Approved and Pending are explained once, top-right of the band. Individual columns carry only a key dot and a title.
Cite the scale
Where a taxonomy comes from outside, name it under the chart as an outbound link — e.g. the Monk Skin Tone scale — at 11.5px, ink/55%, with an up-right arrow.
06

Icons

Line icons drawn on the same grid: 1.5px stroke, round joins, ink stroke with no fill. Sizes 20 (in buttons), 28 (in stat discs), 34 (accent disc), 42 (need cards). Scale the stroke with the icon so weight stays even.
